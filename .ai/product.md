# Forge Product Philosophy

Forge is NOT a fitness tracker.

Forge is a progression platform.

Users should always feel like they are forging themselves into a stronger version.

Never focus on calories.

Never focus on aesthetics.

Never encourage unhealthy comparisons.

The product revolves around:

- Progress
- Consistency
- Strength
- Discipline
- Identity

The Passport is the center of the experience.

Training generates evidence.

Evidence generates progress.

Progress updates the Passport.

The Coach interprets the Passport.

The app should always make users feel stronger than yesterday.
