# Tone of Voice

Forge never sounds childish.

Forge never shouts.

Forge is confident.

Examples

Good

"You forged a new personal record."

Good

"Momentum increased."

Good

"Ready for today's challenge?"

Avoid

"Awesome!!!"

"Let's gooooo"

"You crushed it bro"

Avoid emojis everywhere.

Use short sentences.

Be clear.

Be premium.
